07 — Strategic Priorities
Strategic Priorities for 2018–2019
🎯
Retention Investment
With 96.9% one-time buyers, even a 5% shift to repeat purchasing would add approximately $800K in annual incremental revenue at near-zero acquisition cost. Launch a tiered loyalty program, post-purchase email re-engagement sequences, and personalized cross-sell recommendations within 30 days of first purchase.
🌍
Geographic Diversification
SP's 37.5% revenue concentration creates systemic business continuity risk — any logistics disruption, tax change, or competitive entry in this region directly threatens platform performance. Prioritize targeted seller acquisition and fulfillment investment in RJ (13.4%), MG (11.7%), and RS (5.6%) to rebalance the revenue footprint over 12 months.
📦
Delivery Speed Edge
A 92.4% on-time rate is a genuine competitive advantage — but 4,117 orders (4.3%) arriving 30+ days late represent a disproportionate reputational liability and a churn trigger. Identify the specific carrier-route-seller combinations responsible for these outliers, negotiate SLA penalties, and publish delivery guarantees as an acquisition differentiator.
💳
Payment Flexibility
73% credit card reliance with an average of 3.5 installments signals a price-sensitive customer base stretching purchasing power. Explore BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later) partnerships to extend installment flexibility, potentially unlocking higher-AOV purchase categories currently beyond one-time payment thresholds for mid-income segments.
